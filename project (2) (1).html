Summary statistics are valid only for numerical columns.

survived:

The mean of survived is about 0.384, survived can be either 1 or 0(did survive, did not) which means 38.4% survived.

pclass:

The average passenger class is about 2.31. Pclass ranges from 1 to 3, which means many passengers were in 2nd or 3rd class. The median is 3, at least half of passengers were in 3rd class.

age:

The average age is about 29.7, median age is 28. These are close, so age is not extremely skewed. However, the maximum age is 80, so there are older passengers, which adds outliers.

sibsp:

Most passengers had 0 siblings or spouses aboard, because the median is 0. The maximum is 8, which may be an outlier or an unusually large family group.

parch:

Most passengers also had 0 parents or children aboard, because the median and 75th percentile are both 0. The maximum is 6, which is an outlier.

fare:

The fare column is strongly right-skewed. The median fare is roughly 14.45, but the mean is approximately 32.2, and the maximum is 512.33. This means a few passengers paid very high fares, probably first-class passengers, creating outliers.

# Task 6: Univariate Analysis
plt.figure(figsize=(8, 5))
sns.histplot(diamonds["price"], bins=40, kde=True)
plt.title("Distribution of Diamond Prices")
plt.xlabel("Price")
plt.ylabel("Frequency")
plt.show()

Task 6

Justification:

Histogram is used since price is numeric. It shows how values are distributed across intervals. The KDE line also helps visualize the overall shape of the distribution. (histogram is useful because diamond prices are spread over many values, and we want to see the overall shape.)

Interpretation:

The distribution of diamond prices is right-skewed. Most diamonds have lower to medium prices, while less diamonds have very high prices. The long right tail possible high-price outliers. The center of the data is closer to the lower price range rather than the maximum.
